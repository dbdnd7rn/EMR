# EnVizion Life Caregiver Toolkit — Requirements from Dr. Delphine Tolbert Email

Source: Email from Dr. Delphine Tolbert dated September 8, 2026.

## Program identity

**EnVizion Life Caregiver Toolkit & Patient Advocate Support Program**

Positioning: empowering families to navigate healthcare with faith, clarity, and compassion.

Founder: Dr. Delphine Tolbert, DNP, RN, CLC

Website: envizionlife.net

## Mission

Guide and uplift caregivers with clarity, education, and advocacy while they care for loved ones experiencing acute or chronic illness, with support rooted in faith.

## Target audience

- Adult children caring for aging parents
- Spouses or family members managing chronic illness at home
- Individuals transitioning loved ones from hospital to home
- Faith-based communities, churches, and outreach ministries
- Healthcare systems and discharge teams seeking family-centered resources

## Program components

### 1. Patient Advocate Coaching Services

- One-on-one coaching
- Group coaching
- Spiritual wellness for caregivers
- Insurance and Medicare education
- Advance directive and rights awareness

### 2. Caregiver Toolkit — Print and Digital

The source specifically lists:

- Daily trackers for vital signs
- Blood sugar tracking
- CHF symptom tracking
- Medication logs
- Behavior and delirium monitoring
- Appointment preparation
- Red-flag symptom logs
- Patient rights summary
- Advance directive starter template
- Disease-specific fact sheets

### 3. Medical System Navigation Guides

Guides should explain what to expect from:

- Primary Care Physicians
- Cardiologists — CHF, hypertension, arrhythmias
- Hematologist-Oncologists — cancer, anemia, chemotherapy
- Nephrologists — CKD, dialysis
- Neurologists — stroke, dementia, Parkinson's disease
- Endocrinologists — diabetes, thyroid disorders
- Pulmonologists — COPD, asthma, oxygen needs
- Rheumatologists — lupus, rheumatoid arthritis, autoimmune illness

## Tools and resources

The source specifies:

- Digital version with editable logs
- Printable version for home, church, and hospital use
- QR-code access to reference links such as Medicare, drug lookups, and illness guides
- Transitional care insert: **Walking Through the Transition**

## Safety boundary

The current project remains an education, advocacy, care-coordination, and caregiver-support toolkit. Clinical wording, emergency guidance, rights content, advance-directive content, and disease-specific educational material should be reviewed and approved by EnVizion Life before publication.

## Open product decisions created by this source

- Whether faith / spiritual-support content appears during onboarding, as an optional section, or both
- Whether coaching services are informational only in MVP or include booking / contact flows
- Whether printable exports are required in first release
- Whether blood sugar, CHF, and behavior / delirium tracking are condition-specific or available from a general daily tracker
- Whether the app needs a dedicated hospital-to-home transition journey
- Whether Medicare / insurance education is U.S.-only content
- Whether patient-rights and advance-directive templates require state-specific handling
