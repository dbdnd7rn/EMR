# EnVizion Life Digital Caregiver Toolkit

A mobile-first caregiver support application and administration platform based on EnVizion Life education materials.

## Product goals

- **Educate** — make approved care information easier to understand.
- **Organize** — bring medicines, symptoms, questions, appointments, and care-team details together.
- **Act** — help caregivers recognize meaningful changes and reach approved guidance quickly.

## MVP direction

The first release is planned around condition education, specialist guides, action plans, a health journal, medication tracking, appointment preparation, care-team contacts, emergency tools, and trusted resources.

The product supports education and care coordination. It is not intended to diagnose conditions, independently change prescribed treatment, or replace an individualized care plan.
