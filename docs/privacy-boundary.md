# Privacy boundary — working decision log

## Current rule

Do not persist identifiable caregiver or patient health information until EnVizion Life confirms the data scope and the project has an approved privacy/security design.

## Allowed in the current backend foundation

- approved educational condition content
- specialist guides
- action-plan wording approved by EnVizion Life
- trusted resources and reference links
- content status and version metadata

## Kept prototype-only for now

The following features may be demonstrated in the mobile prototype, but their data should remain local/session-only until the privacy scope is approved:

- health-journal entries
- vital-sign readings
- blood-sugar readings
- symptom logs
- behavior or delirium notes
- medication lists
- appointment notes and questions
- named care-team contacts
- caregiver and care-recipient profiles

## Decisions still required

1. Are caregivers, patients, or both authenticated users?
2. Can one caregiver manage more than one person?
3. Can multiple caregivers share one care recipient?
4. Will identifiable health information be stored?
5. What information is necessary for the MVP?
6. What are the hosting, access, retention, export, and deletion rules?
7. What U.S. healthcare privacy/compliance obligations apply to the deployment model?
8. Who at EnVizion Life approves clinical and educational content before publication?

This boundary follows the proposal’s instruction to define privacy, hosting, access, retention, and healthcare-compliance requirements before launch if identifiable health information is stored.
